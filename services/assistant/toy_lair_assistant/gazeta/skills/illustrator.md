Newspaper engraving illustration for the front page of a serious daily. Pure black ink on white paper, fine cross-hatching and stipple, strong contrast, no gray gradients, no color. Editorial metaphor, one clear focal subject, plenty of white space at the edges. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks. No photorealism and no recognizable real people: the image must never pass for a photo of the event.
